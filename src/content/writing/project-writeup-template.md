## The Setup

What the project was. The constraints, the team, the timeline, the stack, where you were
starting from. Enough context that a reader unfamiliar with the project understands what
follows.

## What I'd Flag

The honest, self-critical throughline. Friction, tradeoffs, what didn't go as planned, or a
tension worth naming. This section exists so the piece doesn't read as a highlight reel.

## Where That Leaves Me

The fact-first landing point. What actually happened, the role or outcome, no interpretive
spin telling the reader what to conclude. End on the last concrete fact and let it stand.
